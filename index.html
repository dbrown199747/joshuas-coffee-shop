About

A proper independent, in the middle of Dorset

Joshua's has been part of Sturminster Newton for years — a genuinely independent café run by people who care about the coffee, the food, and the people who come through the door.

We take the coffee seriously. We make the food fresh every day. And we've got comfortable seats and fast, free WiFi if you need to stay a while.

While you're here

Sturminster Newton

A Dorset market town with more going on than most people expect.

Every first Saturday

Classic Car Show

The town fills with beautifully restored vehicles from across the decades — completely free to attend. Make a morning of it: coffee at ours first, then a wander through the cars.

Sturminster Newton Mill

A working watermill on the River Stour, one of the finest heritage sites in Dorset. Open seasonally with milling demonstrations.

The Monday Market

Centuries old and still going — local produce, crafts, and familiar faces in the market square every Monday.

River Stour Walks

Step out of town onto footpaths through the Blackmore Vale. Some of the best walking in Dorset, starting right here.

Thomas Hardy's House

Hardy lived and wrote here from 1876–78. His former home is preserved and open as a local landmark.

Year-round events

From the Cheese Festival to seasonal fairs and live music — Sturminster Newton's calendar stays busy throughout the year.
