About

A proper
independent café

Joshua's is an independent coffee shop in the heart of Sturminster Newton — a place built around good coffee, honest food, and the kind of welcome you only get somewhere that's genuinely part of its community.

We take our coffee seriously (award-winning seriously), our lunches are made fresh every day, and there's always a seat and free WiFi if you need to stay a while.

While you're here

Sturminster Newton

A Dorset market town with more going on than you might expect.

Every first Saturday

Classic Car Show

On the first Saturday of every month, Sturminster Newton fills up with beautifully restored classic cars from across the decades. It's completely free to attend and a brilliant reason to make a morning of it — pop in for a coffee first.

The Old Mill

A working watermill on the River Stour, one of Dorset's finest heritage sites. Open seasonally with milling demonstrations.

Monday Market

A centuries-old tradition in the market square — local produce, crafts, and familiar faces every Monday.

River Stour Walks

Step out from the town onto miles of footpaths through the Blackmore Vale. The Stour valley is as good as Dorset gets.

Thomas Hardy's Home

Hardy lived here from 1876–78, calling it some of his happiest years. His former home is preserved as a local landmark.

Cheese Festival & More

The town's calendar includes the famous Cheese Festival plus seasonal fairs, live music, and community events year-round.
