About Sturminster Newton

Sturminster Newton is a picturesque market town in the Blackmore Vale, nestled alongside the River Stour in the heart of Dorset — and it's well worth a wander after your coffee.

🚗

Classic Car Show

Every first Saturday of the month, the town hosts a wonderful classic car show — a fantastic free event showcasing beautifully restored vehicles from across the decades. Perfect before or after a visit to us!

🏭

Sturminster Newton Mill

Visit the historic working watermill on the River Stour, one of Dorset's finest heritage sites. Open seasonally with fascinating demonstrations of traditional milling.

😊

Market Days

The Monday market has been a fixture in Sturminster Newton for centuries. Browse local produce, crafts, and more in the traditional market square.

🌿

Country Walks

The town sits within the stunning Blackmore Vale, offering beautiful walking routes along the River Stour and across the surrounding Dorset countryside.

🏭

Thomas Hardy Connection

The renowned author Thomas Hardy lived in Sturminster Newton between 1876–1878. Visit his former home, now preserved as a local landmark.

🎁

Local Events

From the Sturminster Newton Cheese Festival to seasonal fairs, the town's calendar is packed with community events throughout the year.
